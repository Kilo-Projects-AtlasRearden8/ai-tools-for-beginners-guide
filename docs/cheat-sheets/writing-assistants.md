# Writing Assistants — Quick Reference

## What is a writing assistant?
AI that helps you write — emails, letters, resumes, posts. Gives you a starting point; you edit to make it yours.

## Free tools to try
- Any chatbot (Gemini, Claude, Copilot)
- **Grammarly** — grammarly.com (grammar/clarity)
- **Hemingway Editor** — hemingwayapp.com (highlights hard sentences)
- **Google Docs** — docs.google.com ("Help me write" feature)

## Step-by-step: first email
1. Open a chatbot or Google Docs
2. Tell it what to write: "Write a short, polite email to my landlord about a leaky faucet dripping for 3 days"
3. Read the draft — AI can sound stiff
4. Edit until it sounds like YOU
5. Copy to your email and send

## Tips
- Give context: who, what, tone
- Set tone: "polite but firm," "friendly and casual"
- Ask for options: "Three subject lines"
- Read aloud before sending
- Never send medical/legal/financial claims without checking

## Keep your voice
- Swap in words you actually use
- Add a personal detail only you'd know
- Cut robot-sounding sentences
- Sign off your way

## Try now
- "Polite email declining party invite — working that night, 3 sentences"
- "Cover letter: part-time library assistant, customer service experience, love books"
- "Rewrite this text kinder: [paste]"
- "Back-cover blurb: cozy mystery in small Indiana town"

# Chatbots — Quick Reference

## What is a chatbot?
An AI program you talk to by typing. Like texting a well-read friend who sometimes makes things up.

## Free chatbots to try
- **Google Gemini** — gemini.google.com
- **Claude** — claude.ai
- **Microsoft Copilot** — copilot.microsoft.com
- **Perplexity** — perplexity.ai (shows sources)

## Step-by-step: first chat
1. Open gemini.google.com (or any above)
2. Sign in with your email
3. Type a question in the box at bottom
4. Press Enter
5. Ask follow-ups: "Make it shorter," "Give me a list"

## Tips for better answers
- Be specific: "Write a thank-you note to my aunt for $50, keep it warm"
- Say who it's for: "Explain like I'm new to computers"
- One thing at a time
- Say what you DON'T want: "No medical terms"
- Ask for sources: "Where did you get this?"

## Watch out for
- Chatbots can be confident and wrong — double-check important facts
- Don't share passwords, bank info, or health details
- Chats may be saved — check settings for "turn off history"

## Try now
- "Five easy breakfasts under 10 minutes"
- "Rewrite this email polite but firm: [paste]"
- "Explain credit scores like I've never heard of them"

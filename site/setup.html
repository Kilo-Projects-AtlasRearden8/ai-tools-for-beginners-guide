Step-by-step setup

You do not need a new computer, a fancy phone, or technical skills to start using AI tools. If you can browse the web and send an email, you are ready.

What you need

  • A device: a computer, tablet, or smartphone
  • A web browser: Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox (any modern browser works)
  • An email address you own and can check
  • About 20 minutes

Step 1: Make sure you have an email address

Most AI tools ask you to sign in. If you already have an email (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, etc.), you are done. If not, create a free one at gmail.com or outlook.com. Write your email and password down on paper and keep it somewhere safe.

Step 2: Pick one tool to start with

Don't try everything at once. Choose one:

  • Chatbots: gemini.google.com, claude.ai, or copilot.microsoft.com
  • Image generators: designer.microsoft.com or firefly.adobe.com
  • Audio tools: the Read Aloud feature in Microsoft Edge, or voice typing in Google Docs

Open that site in your browser.

Step 3: Create a free account

  1. Look for "Sign in," "Get started," or "Try for free."
  2. Enter your email address.
  3. Create a password. Make it something you can remember but hard for others to guess. Consider writing it down in a notebook you keep at home.
  4. If the site sends a confirmation email, open your email and click the link.
  5. Some sites ask for a phone number to verify you are a real person. You can skip this on some sites; on others it is required.

Step 4: Bookmark the site

Once you are signed in, add the site to your browser's bookmarks so you can find it again:

  • Chrome/Edge: click the star icon in the address bar, or press Ctrl+D (Windows) / Cmd+D (Mac).
  • Safari: click the Share button, then "Add Bookmark."
  • Firefox: click the star icon in the address bar.

Step 5: Try your first task

Pick something small and low-stakes:

  • Ask a chatbot, "What's a good weeknight dinner with chicken?"
  • Ask an image generator, "A sunny garden with blue flowers, watercolor style."
  • Use Read Aloud in Edge to have a webpage read to you.

You have now used an AI tool. Everything else is just practice.

Keeping track of your accounts

It helps to keep a simple list on paper at home:

Tool Website Email used Password hint
Example: Google Gemini gemini.google.com myname@gmail.com (write a hint, not the full password)

Never keep actual passwords in a file on your computer or phone unless it is a password manager you trust. A paper notebook in a drawer is often safer for beginners.

If something goes wrong

  • Forgot your password? Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and follow the email steps.
  • Site won't load? Try refreshing the page or using a different browser.
  • Not sure what to type? Start with "Hello" and ask the chatbot what it can help you with.
  • You don't have to keep using any tool. You can close the tab, delete your account in the settings, and walk away at any time.

What comes next

Once you are comfortable with one tool, try another. Each page in this manual walks you through one kind of tool. When you are ready, read the safety and privacy basics before you explore further.

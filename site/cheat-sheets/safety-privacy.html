Safety & Privacy — Quick Reference

Golden rules

  1. Never share secrets — no passwords, bank numbers, SSN, health details
  2. Assume chats are saved — don't write what you wouldn't want a stranger to read
  3. AI can be wrong — invents facts, names, dates, medical info. Double-check important things
  4. AI is not professional advice — not a doctor, lawyer, pharmacist, accountant. Never medical advice.
  5. You can leave anytime — close tab, delete account, stop using

Privacy settings to check

After signing in, look under Settings/Privacy/Data controls for: - Turn off chat history — conversations not saved - Don't train on my data — company doesn't use your chats to improve AI - Delete my data/account — remove your info

Names vary by site. If you can't find them, just don't share personal details.

Scams to know

  • Fake AI voices: scammer copies voice from short clip, calls pretending to be family asking money. Hang up, call back on known number.
  • Fake AI support: real companies never ask for password/full credit card over chat
  • Fake images/stories: AI makes fake pictures of events that never happened. Check source before believing.

Health: extra caution

AI explains general topics but doesn't know YOUR history, meds, body. Never use AI to: - Diagnose a symptom - Change medication/dose - Decide to skip a doctor appointment

When in doubt: call your nurse, doctor, or pharmacist. AI is education only.

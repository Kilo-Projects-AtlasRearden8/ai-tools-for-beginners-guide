Image Generators — Quick Reference

What is an image generator?

An AI tool that makes pictures from words. Type a description (prompt), get an image. No drawing needed.

Free tools to try

  • Microsoft Designer — designer.microsoft.com (free, DALL-E powered)
  • Adobe Firefly — firefly.adobe.com (free tier)
  • Leonardo.ai — leonardo.ai (daily free credits)
  • Craiyon — craiyon.com (free, no account needed)
  • Ideogram — ideogram.ai (good at text in images)

Step-by-step: first image

  1. Open designer.microsoft.com (or any above)
  2. Sign in if asked
  3. Find the prompt box ("Describe the image...")
  4. Type: "A golden retriever puppy sleeping in sunlight, watercolor"
  5. Click Generate
  6. Click an image to enlarge, then download

Prompt tips

  • Describe what you see: subject, setting, style
  • Name a style: watercolor, oil painting, sketch, photo, cartoon
  • Add mood: "golden hour," "soft light," "moody"
  • Keep people simple at first (faces/hands can look odd)
  • Try again — small word changes = big differences

Watch out for

  • Copyright is unclear for AI art — check terms if selling
  • AI may add weird details — look before sharing
  • Don't make images of real people without permission
  • Don't make images to fool people (fake news, IDs)

Try now

  • "Cozy corner with tea, book, morning light, watercolor"
  • "Orange cat in astronaut helmet, stars, digital art"
  • "Coloring page: garden with flowers, butterfly, thick outlines"
  • "Vintage postcard: small town main street, bakery, flower shop"
